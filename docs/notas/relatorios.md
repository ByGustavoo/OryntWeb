# Relatórios: Resumo, Exportação e Comparação

* Relatórios › Resumo (`PaginaResumoRelatorio`) shows closed calendar periods only: a day, a week from Monday to Sunday or a month, never the one in progress (`REQUISITOS.md`, "Relatórios: Resumo"). `periodoRelatorio.ts` owns that: a period is `{ granularidade, inicio }` with `inicio` as a local `AAAA-MM-DD` date, valid only when aligned, already closed and inside the last 12 months (`lerPeriodo`). Dates are compared as strings, so keep that format.
* The report period never goes to the URL (the user asked on 11 October 2026 for `/relatorios/resumo` with no query string). It lives in the history state (`usePeriodoRelatorio`, used by Resumo and Exportação): `mudarPeriodo` navigates to the same path with `replace` and `state: { periodo }`, so a reload keeps the period and opening the address again starts at the last closed week. The dashboard shortcut ("Ver o resumo de ontem") and the "Exportar" button of Resumo pass the period the same way, through the navigation `state`. Anything read from that state goes through `lerPeriodo` first.
* "Comparar", "Exportar" and "Imprimir" of Resumo live in `BarraPeriodo`, on the row of the Dia, Semana and Mês control (the user asked on 12 October 2026), through its `acoes` prop; Exportação passes none. The bar is a container (`barra-periodo`) with three modes, so the row never changes height while the user steps through the periods: from 1150px the buttons show their labels, from 930px to 1149px only the icons (the label stays for screen readers and the `title` is the tooltip), and below 930px they take a row of their own, as three equal columns without icons below 400px. The two limits come from the widest common case, a week that crosses two months with "Mais recente" showing: 1146px with labels and 923px with icons. At 1280px with the menu open the bar has 949px, so that width shows the icons; measure again before adding a fourth action or a longer label. Comparação takes the same `acoes` list in `SeletorComparacao`, which puts "Imprimir" at the right end of the row of its size control (`.topo`); with one button the label always fits, and only at 320px it wraps under the control. `.topo` carries `nao-imprimir` itself, so the printed sheet does not keep an empty row.
* The report is one request (`GET /relatorios/resumo`) and one DTO, so its blocks cannot disagree. The blocks take the period from the DTO (`dados.granularidade`, `dados.inicio`), not from the period being requested: do not pass the page's `periodo` down to them. `relatorios.test.ts` enforces that the total equals the sum of the chart, that the slow-query count matches the table and the performance band, and that the three bands add up to every query, every execution and 100% of the time.
* The report sentences ("Destaques") are a pure function of the DTO (`montarDestaques`, tested string by string). A drop in the average time is `positivo` and a rise is `atencao`; volume carries no judgement. The copied text (`textoDosDestaques`) ends its first line with "(dados demonstrativos)" on a simulated connection: keep it, it is the only notice that leaves the app with the text.
* The two charts of "Evolução no período" are small multiples on purpose (no dual axis): a `BarChart` of volume over an `AreaChart` of average time, with the same margins and y-axis width. The area chart uses the default point scale with a measured `padding` of half a band (`useLarguraElemento`), which puts its points and ticks under the centre of the bars. Do not swap that for `scale="band"`: Recharts then leaves the ticks at the start of each band. The peak labels have a surface-colored halo (`paint-order: stroke`) so the gridlines do not cross them.
* The performance bands use their own chart tokens (`--desempenho-rapida`, `--desempenho-moderada`, `--desempenho-lenta` in `temas.css`), not the `--sucesso` / `--aviso` / `--erro` family: those failed the dataviz palette validator as marks side by side (amber against red is 2.0 under deuteranopia on the light surface). The six values pass it on `#111111` and `#ffffff`; revalidate when changing them. Text never takes these colors: the band name sits beside a swatch.
* Printing: "Imprimir" goes through `useImpressao` (`src/ganchos/`), which shows the button as loading, waits 50ms for that to paint and then calls `window.print()`, because the call blocks the page until the dialog closes (the user said on 12 October 2026 that the button froze). The hook takes the screen name and the subject (`useImpressao('Resumo', '5 a 11 de outubro de 2026')`) and builds two texts from them. On `beforeprint` it swaps `document.title` for the name the PDF is saved with ("Orynt - Resumo - 5 a 11 de outubro de 2026"), writes the running header ("Resumo · 5 a 11 de outubro de 2026") as a CSS string in `--folha-identificacao` on the root and stamps the time shown as "Gerado em"; on `afterprint` it puts the title back and removes the property. The screen theme never changes for printing (the user reported the page turning light on 12 October 2026): in `temas.css` the light palette is the base (`:root, [data-tema='claro']`) and the dark one sits inside `@media screen`, so paper always gets the light tokens and `ProvedorTema` has no print code. Keep both blocks with the same list of tokens and never move the dark block out of `@media screen`. The sheet opens with `CabecalhoImpressao` (`src/componentes/relatorios/`, the user asked on 12 October 2026 for the logo and the name on the PDF): the mark and the name, "Relatórios" and the screen name, a rule that starts in the accent blue, the period, what it is compared with and the connection. It exists only on paper and takes the period from the DTO, not from the one being requested; `CabecalhoPagina` (`imprimir={false}`), `BarraPeriodo` and `SeletorComparacao` are hidden there. `@page` in `global.css` sets the margins and writes the slogan and "Página 1 de 2" in the bottom margin boxes and the running header in `@top-left` (Chrome 131 or newer). Chrome drops its own footer or header on a page only when that edge has a margin box, which is why the top one exists (the user said on 12 October 2026 that the document title, which Chrome centers in the top margin, looked loose on the sheet): `@top-left` reads `--folha-identificacao` and falls back to an empty string, so a screen without `useImpressao` prints a clean top, and `@page :first` sets it to `''` because the first page already opens with `CabecalhoImpressao`. Never `content: none` there: with no box Chrome writes its date and title again on that page. The two report pages print at `zoom: var(--escala-folha)` (0.76): with the 9mm side margins an A4 sheet gets 955px of layout width, so the container queries pick the desktop layout, four indicators in a row and real tables instead of cards, and the report fits in two pages; change the zoom and the margins together and check that width again. `global.css` turns animations and transitions off in print, keeps background colors (`print-color-adjust`) and hides `.nao-imprimir` and the skip link (as a fixed element it was printed on every page). In print the dividers of `GradeIndicadores` are borders, not the 1px gaps of the screen, which went missing at that zoom, and the tables show the whole SQL and only the arrow of the sorted column. The charts are scaled by CSS in print (the `@media print` block of `GraficoEvolucao.module.css`), because `ResizeObserver` does not run for the print layout. The print dialog cannot be opened by the Chrome extension: replace `window.print` in the page to check the button, and make the real PDF with a headless Chrome driven by `Page.printToPDF`, which is the only way to see page breaks and the margin boxes.
* The three report screens show a skeleton while another period loads (the user asked on 12 October 2026; until then the previous report stayed on screen dimmed). Each page compares the period of the DTO with the requested one (`trocandoDePeriodo`, `trocandoDePeriodos` in Comparação), with no `carregando` in it, as in Consultas SQL › Todas, and the `atualizando` dimming is left for a reload of the same period. The skeleton keeps the frames and the real titles: `PainelEmEspera` (`EsqueletoRelatorio.tsx`) is a `Painel` with the real `CabecalhoPainel`, and each block exports its own skeleton from its own file, beside the title it shares with it (`EsqueletoDestaques`, `EsqueletoDistribuicao`, `EsqueletoDesempenhoComparado`, `EsqueletoConsultasComparadas`, `EsqueletoPrevia`), as `EsqueletoIndicadores` already did. `RelatorioEmEspera` sets `--espera-esqueleto: 0ms` on a change of period, so the skeleton comes without the 300ms wait; the first load uses the same skeleton with the wait. The heights are the measured ones of the default week at 1320px of container (charts 190 and 180, or 180 and 200 with the legend; table rows of 66 and 74; `linhasPorFrase` `[1, 1, 1, 3]` and `[2, 2, 1, 3, 3]` for the sentences), which keeps every block within 4px of the loaded one there: measure again after changing a block. `LinhaEmEspera` is a bar centred in a box of `1lh`, so a skeleton line takes the line height of the class it is given. Exportação is different on purpose: "O que exportar" is a form that does not depend on the period, so it stays mounted and usable (`CorpoExportacao` takes `dados` or `null` and is no longer inside `ConteudoAssincrono`, which would remount it when the first answer arrives), and only the row counts and the preview turn into skeleton, with "Baixar" loading and the copy buttons `aria-disabled` so that no file leaves with the previous period.
* Relatórios › Exportação has no API route of its own (`REQUISITOS.md`, "Relatórios: Exportação"): it reads the same `GET /relatorios/resumo` and `exportacaoRelatorio.ts` builds the files in the browser, so a file can never disagree with the Resumo screen. Everything goes through one table model (`montarTabela`: columns typed `TEXTO`, `CODIGO`, `INTEIRO` or `DECIMAL`), and the CSV, the Markdown and the copied table are three writers over it; the JSON repeats the DTO field names. A new block or column goes into `montarTabela`, not into a writer.
* The CSV is written for Excel in Portuguese by default: byte order mark, CRLF, `;` between columns and decimal comma (`BRASILEIRO`), or `,` and decimal point (`INTERNACIONAL`). Dates stay `AAAA-MM-DD` and numbers carry no thousands separator in both. A text cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets an apostrophe in front (`neutralizarFormula`), because the SQL text comes from the database and a spreadsheet would run it as a formula; numbers are never touched, so a negative variation stays a number. Keep that guard in the CSV and in the copied table.
* Exported demo data says so inside the file, not only in its name: an "Origem dos dados" column on every row of the CSV and of the copied table, `dadosDemonstrativos` and `aviso` in the JSON, a quote at the top of the Markdown. It is the same rule as the "(dados demonstrativos)" of the copied sentences: the notice has to survive a rename.
* The block list of Exportação follows the short-list order (Evolução, Consultas, Desempenho, Indicadores, in `blocosExportacao`) and the preview tabs follow it too, while the files themselves keep the order of the report (`ordemNoRelatorio`: indicators, evolution, performance, queries). Do not merge the two lists.
* Relatórios › Comparação has no API route of its own either (`REQUISITOS.md`, "Relatórios: Comparação"): `servicoRelatorios.buscarComparacao` reads `GET /relatorios/resumo` twice and waits for both, so the page always gets the two reports together, and `comparacaoRelatorio.ts` computes every difference. Its blocks take the two periods from the DTOs (`periodosDaComparacao`), not from the pair being requested, for the same reason as in Resumo.
* The Comparação numbers must match the Resumo ones when the base is the previous period: `variacaoEntre` rounds to one decimal from the values as the DTO carries them, and the simulator computes its own variations the same way (it used the unrounded average time until 11 October 2026, which made the two screens differ by 0.2 point). `relatorios.test.ts` enforces it for the total, the average time and each query; keep the contract note about it in `docs/api-contrato.md`.
* The pair of periods of Comparação lives in the history state as `{ periodo, base }` (`useComparacaoRelatorio`), read through `lerPeriodo` and `lerBase`: the base has the same size as the period and is never equal to it. Moving the period carries the base at the same distance (`acompanharPeriodo`), changing the size resets it to the previous period, and the base arrows jump over the period (`vizinhaDaBase`). The "Comparar" button of Resumo sends only `periodo`, so the base starts as the previous one.
* Two labels name each period of Comparação and they are not interchangeable: `rotulosDaComparacao` (short, `28/09 a 04/10`, lowercase month names, used inside sentences such as "em agosto") and `titulosDaComparacao` (the same with a capital letter, for legends, bar labels and table headers). Both add the year only when the two periods touch different years. Inside the sentences the base is cited through `referenciaDaBase` ("na semana de 21 a 27 de setembro", "em 9 de outubro", "em agosto"). The two sides of `SeletorComparacao` take a third pair, `rotulosDoSeletor` (the user asked on 12 October 2026 for the period to have the same size under "Período" and under "Comparar com"): the short form, always with the year (`05/10 a 11/10/2026`), in tabular figures, so in Dia and Semana the two texts have exactly the same width, which the long form could not give when only one of the weeks crossed two months. When one of the two weeks crosses the year, both sides write the year at both ends (`22/12/2025 a 28/12/2025`). Mês keeps the month name. The long form stays in a visually hidden span for screen readers, and the visible one is `aria-hidden`. The two sides are two separate cards with the invert button in the gap between them (the user said on 12 October 2026 that the left one looked bigger): as one card split by a divider the two halves measured the same, but the content of the right half started 42px after the divider and the left one 25px after the border. Each `.lado` now carries its own border and the same padding on both sides; stacked (up to 760px of `seletor-comparacao`) the rows are `1fr auto 1fr`, so the cards keep the same height when the shortcuts of the second one wrap at 320px. Do not merge them back into one card.
* The two charts of "Evolução lado a lado" are line charts aligned by position (`alinharEvolucao`): hour, weekday or day of the month, so the shorter month ends earlier and its points are `null`. The period is a solid line in `--grafico-1` and the base a dashed line in `--grafico-referencia`, a gray on purpose (one series is the point, the other is context): the dataviz validator fails its chroma floor by design and passes the separation and contrast checks against `--grafico-1` on `#111111` and `#ffffff` (ΔE 17.8 and 18.1 under protanopia, 3:1 against the surface). The dash is the second encoding; the same two strokes mark "Período" and "Comparar com" in `SeletorComparacao`, the legend, the tooltip and the base value of each indicator, so change them together.
* The query table of Comparação is not `TabelaConsultas`: it has its own columns (the two average times, the variation with a small diverging bar centred on zero, the executions) and turns into cards below 860px of container width; the performance table turns into cards below 560px. That table sits beside its bars in a grid whose second column is `minmax(min-content, 1.1fr)`, and its period headers may wrap: with `minmax(0, …)` and `nowrap` headers the table kept its 573px inside a 459px column and the page scrolled sideways at 1040px (fixed on 12 October 2026). The widest case is a week that crosses the year (`29/12/2025 a 04/01/2026`), which needs 537px with the headers wrapped; the 560px limit of the cards comes from it, so measure that week again before changing either number. The filter labels carry no count on purpose ("Todas", "Pioraram", "Melhoraram" with counts overflowed the panel at 320px); the counts are in the description of the panel (`resumirTendencias`).
* Several CSV files are downloaded one after the other (`baixarArquivos`, 250ms apart); Chrome asks once whether the site may download multiple files, and the success notification says what to do when only the first one arrives. Checking this screen through the Chrome extension must not save files on the machine: replace `HTMLAnchorElement.prototype.click` and `URL.createObjectURL` in the page first and read the names and sizes from there.
