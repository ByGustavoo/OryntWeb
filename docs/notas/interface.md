# Interface: shell, design system e dashboard

Decisões e armadilhas do shell, do menu, do cabeçalho, das configurações, dos componentes de `ui/`, dos tokens, do dashboard e das revisões gerais de interface.

* The interface shows no demo notice since 22 October 2026 (`REQUISITOS.md`, "Refinamento de 22 de outubro de 2026", asked by the user): no "Dados demonstrativos" notice or badge, no "Demonstração" badge in the account menu, no "Conexão simulada" note, no demo sentence in notifications. Do not add one back on a new screen. What stays on purpose: the login credentials card, the simulated inboxes of the recovery and of the invite (the flows need them), the dev-only scenario panel and the marks inside exported files and copied text ("Origem dos dados", `-demonstracao`, `dadosDemonstrativos`, "(dados demonstrativos)"), so a file with fictitious numbers does not pass as real outside the Orynt. `ConexaoDTO.simulada` still exists and drives those file marks and keeps a simulated change from being announced as "Os comandos rodaram no banco".
* The connection modal never opens by itself: only a click opens it (`abrir` in `ProvedorNovaConexao`), from "Conectar ao banco" on the empty dashboard, "Nova conexão" in the header and the saved connections. `ModalConexao` is lazy and keeps the typed fields between openings, except the password; after a successful connection the next opening remounts it (`geracao`) so the form starts empty.
* Switching connections keeps the current one on screen until the new one answers (`alvo` is the one being activated). `DashboardConectado` is keyed by the connection id, so the data reloads on a switch.
* The modal actions live in the `Modal` footer and submit the form through the `form` attribute; do not move them back inside `FormularioConexao`.
* `Modal` records the element to give focus back to only when the focus is outside its panel, so a modal mounted already open (rename, the lazy connection modal) survives the double effect of StrictMode.
* The UI review of 20 October 2026 (asked by the user: composition, empty areas, modals and the plan tree): `PontosDeAtencao` shows the three most urgent points and a "Mostrar os outros N pontos" toggle when there are more than four (`PONTOS_A_MOSTRAR`, `PONTOS_SEM_RECOLHER`), so the list of Papéis, Permissões, Sessões, Índices and Tabelas starts in the first screen; the expanded state is plain state of the panel and its skeleton draws the same three items plus the toggle. Sessões is the only screen whose points change with the clock, so its skeleton (`[3, 1, 2, 1, 3]`) follows the case seen on 20 October 2026 (394px against 394px at 1920px). The long detail modals (Tabelas, Funções, Índices, Views, Papéis and Sessões) open with `IndiceDoDetalhe` (`objetos/DetalheObjeto.tsx`): a sticky row of section chips read from the direct children of the body that carry `data-indice` (a `SecaoCopiavel` takes it through `indice`), shown from four sections, with the section in view marked by `aria-current` from the scroll of the modal content and a border once it sticks; a chip scrolls the modal content smoothly and moves the focus to the heading without scrolling. Never call `scrollIntoView` inside it: it cancels the smooth scroll of the modal content, which is why the active chip is brought into view by `scrollTo` on the chip row, whose edges fade when it overflows. `Modal` exposes its side padding as `--recuo-modal` for that row. The table detail is the only object detail on `tamanho="xl"` (12 sections, the growth chart and the columns). In Exportação the preview stretches to the height of the form (`align-items: stretch` and `contain: size` on the preview panel, so its content never sets the row height) and shows 24 lines; below 900px of `exportacao` it goes back to its own height. `TabelaIntervalos` gives the interval column 30% and the executions bar up to 18rem, which turned the empty middle into a bar column. The two compared plans stretch to the same height. In Planos de execução the left column stretches to the height of the row and its last card (`.extras > :last-child`) takes the rest, so "Mudanças de plano" ends on the line of the step detail (asked on 20 October 2026); the border of the step detail lives on `.fixo`, the box that scrolls, not on the panel inside it, so its bottom edge stays visible while its content scrolls. The first header cell of the performance table of Comparação (`rowSpan`) aligns with the second header row. The permission editor of the accounts (`EditorDePermissoes`, shared by `ModalConta` and `DetalheConta`) is one bordered list: each group is a row with its title in an 11rem column (a floated `legend`, from 560px of `editor-permissoes`) and one line per permission, with the origin ("Do perfil", "Fora do perfil", "Concedida à parte" in `--info`, "Revogada do perfil" in `--aviso`) on the right and the "Vai conceder"/"Vai revogar" badge only on a pending change; the "Concedida"/"Negada" badge was dropped because it repeated the checkbox (asked on 20 October 2026, the user found the modal disorganized). Below 380px the origin goes under the description. The account detail no longer repeats the e-mail in the modal description (it says the profile and that the account is not a database role), and "Excluir" sits apart at the end of the actions. The event detail drops the module and type badges, which repeated its measures, and shows the exact date beside the relative one. The key-value rows (`.pares`) moved from `DetalheEvento.module.css` to `DetalheObjeto.module.css`, and the change detail uses them for "Quem e onde", whose e-mail was cut in a measure. `CabecalhoPagina` lets the description reach 96ch and grows the text block (`flex: 1 1 24rem`), so the descriptions fit one line beside the actions at 1920px. `html` has `scrollbar-gutter: stable`, so a short page does not move sideways against a long one, and `useTravarRolagem` skips its padding when the gutter is reserved.
* The composition redesign of 22 October 2026 (`REQUISITOS.md`, "Redesenho das composições", asked by the user because every screen looked like the same template): the demo notice it first gave every page (`AvisoDemonstracao`) was removed the same day with the other demo notices (see the gotcha about them); `RevisaoAlteracao` keeps its `Nota`. Views, Índices, Tabelas and Funções open with `Panorama`: a figure of their own on the left and `PontosDeAtencao variante="lateral"` on the right (from 960px of `panorama`; below it the rail goes under the figure, and without points the figure takes the whole width). The lateral items are one button each, with the sentence clamped to two lines (the whole sentence is in the detail and in the accessible name); the collapse to three points still applies. Each figure opens the detail with the `PAINEL` origin, so the table does not scroll its row into view and the focus comes back to the figure row; the Índices figure filters the list instead (situation and table, toggled, scrolling to the list). Sessões puts its points beside `CadeiasDeBloqueio` when there is a chain, through the same `Panorama`; Permissões uses `variante="recolhida"`, a strip with the count and the three most urgent, so the tool starts in the first screen. The figures draw bars in plain HTML (`FiguraDoPanorama.module.css`: `.linha` rows of name, bar and value, 2px gaps between segments, the breakdown of a bar in a `Dica` anchored on the bar), and a page CSS module that overrides `.linha` columns must repeat the narrow rule (`@container figura-panorama (max-width: 520px)`), because its selector is more specific than the shared one. Names inside a figure use `:global(.codigo)` for the ellipsis: a plain `.codigo` in a CSS module is hashed and matched nothing. Colors: dados, índices and TOAST, and the two parts of the function time, use `--categoria-1`, `--categoria-2` and `--categoria-3` (new in `temas.css`, the first three slots of the dataviz reference palette, validated in both themes; the light aqua is below 3:1, so the values are always written beside it); the index situations use `--grafico-1`, `--desempenho-moderada` (the duplicates hatched), `--desempenho-lenta` and `--grafico-referencia` last, because `--aviso-grafico` beside `--erro-grafico` failed the validator (deutan 2.0). Auditoria and Alterações are `LinhaDoTempo` lists (day headers sticky under the app header, `data-evento` and `data-alteracao` on the `li`, so `focoAoFechar` looks for `[data-evento="…"] button`, not `tr`); the old `TabelaAuditoria` and `TabelaAlteracoes` were removed. The audit marks success in neutral gray and shows the result badge only when it failed or was denied. Administração › Usuários puts `PerfisDasContas` beside the list from 1040px of `diretorio-contas`; a profile toggles the `perfil` filter. Planos de execução lost `.abertura`: `IndicadoresPlano` is a full-width row under the query, and `DiagnosticoPlano` is the first card of `.extras`, under the tree. The attention skeleton heights measured before this date were for the full-width panel; measure the lateral rail again before relying on them.
* Tooltips are `Dica` (`componentes/ui/Dica.tsx`, 22 October 2026, asked by the user because the native `title` boxes were unreadable): it clones its single child, so the child must take a ref (`Botao` and `BotaoIcone` forward it) and a `key` in a list goes on the `Dica`, not on the child. The balloon is a portal on `body` with `position: fixed` and `--camada-dica` (68, above modals and floating panels, below notifications), measured in a layout effect: it prefers `lado` (`cima`, `baixo` or `direita`, the side menu in rail mode), flips when there is no room, clamps 8px inside the viewport and moves the arrow to keep pointing at the trigger. It opens after 380ms with the mouse, at once when another tooltip closed less than 400ms before, at once on `:focus-visible`, and on a tap only for triggers that are not inside a control (a tap on a row button opens the row, not the tooltip); it closes on leave, blur, Esc (which then does not reach the modal), scroll, resize and a pointer down outside. `soSeCortado` shows it only when the trigger (or its `[data-corte]` child) overflows, which is how every truncated name and description uses it; `descrever` (implied by `focavel`) links a hidden copy through `aria-describedby`, which the blocked buttons use for their reason. An empty `conteudo` renders the child alone. `BotaoIcone` shows its `rotulo` by default and takes `dica`/`tituloDaDica` for another text, `Selo` takes `titulo`, and there is no `title` attribute left in the components: do not add one. The colors are `--dica-fundo`, `--dica-borda`, `--dica-texto`, `--dica-texto-secundario` and `--dica-sombra` in `temas.css`, and the Recharts tooltips (`.dica` of `GraficoEvolucao`, `GraficoAtividade`) use the same tokens. Second review the same day (the tooltips still covered what was being read): inside a `tr` (or an element with `data-dica-linha`) up to 96px tall, the vertical sides measure from the row, not the trigger, so the tooltip never covers the row being read; `lado` also takes `esquerda`; `[data-dica-ancora]` inside the trigger is the box it points at and `[data-dica-limite]` around that box is the room it must fit in to stay on a side (the figures of Tabelas and Funções wrap the whole row button and point at the last bar segment, inside the bar column, so the tooltip lands on the empty track and flips over the bar when it does not fit; keep that tooltip to a few short lines, a wide one fell back above the row and covered the previous bar). It is hoverable (WCAG 1.4.13): leaving the trigger closes it after 140ms, entering the balloon cancels that, and a pointer down inside it does not close it. The balloon redefines `--texto`, `--texto-secundario`, `--texto-terciario` and `--superficie` to the `--dica-*` tokens, and the Recharts `.dica` classes of `GraficoEvolucao` and `GraficoAtividade` do the same, because the light tooltip is dark now (`--dica-fundo` #1c1f25) and anything inside it that reads a page token would vanish.
* `CampoBusca` takes an `atalho` (the list uses `/`, asked on 12 October 2026): the listener is on `document` and ignores the key inside a field (`aceitaDigitacao`) and while a dialog or a listbox is open (`haCamadaAberta`), and the `kbd` hint shows only while the field is empty, unfocused and on a device with hover.
* On the dashboard the "Consultas lentas" card is a link to that list, filtered, in the same period (`destino` of `IndicadorNumerico`, only when the count is above zero), and "Consultas que mais pesam" has "Ver todas". The card link is the label itself, stretched over the card by `::after`, with an arrow at the end of the label row that moves on hover. "Conexões ativas" became a link to Sessões the same way on 19 October 2026 (S2 of Sessões); the other two cards still get no pointer and no arrow.
* The dashboard data is deterministic: `series.ts` seeds every interval with its own start time, and the indicators are derived from the same series and query list. `series.test.ts` enforces that the total equals the sum of the chart and that the slow-query count matches the table.
* `VariacaoIndicador` colors the arrow and the percentage only when it gets `favoravel` (`'ALTA'` or `'QUEDA'`, the direction that counts as good). On the dashboard both cards have it: "Consultas executadas" is green when it rises and red when it falls (the user asked on 12 October 2026), "Tempo médio de execução" the opposite. The "Consultas executadas" card of Resumo follows the dashboard since 12 October 2026 (the user asked for the red and green arrow there too), so `IndicadoresRelatorio` passes `favoravel` to both cards, and `IndicadoresComparacao` does the same through the `favoravel` prop of its `Mudanca` (`'ALTA'` for the volume, `'QUEDA'` for the average time, the slow queries and, since 12 October 2026, the connection peak: the user asked for red and green on every variation arrow of the project, and the direction of the peak was picked as a default, a rise is red because connections have a limit). Every variation arrow now carries a judgement, so a new one needs its `favoravel` too. In `DestaquesRelatorio` the tile of the `PIORA` sentence is red (`.piora`, `--erro` on `--erro-suave`) instead of the amber of the `atencao` tone, which the other sentences keep. The volume stays neutral only in the sentences of "Destaques" and of "O que mudou". The `%` is a separate span in the interface font: the Geist Mono glyph was hard to read at 12px.
* The indicator card (`IndicadorNumerico`, shared by the dashboard, Resumo and Comparação) draws its icon at 18px inside a 32px tile (the user asked on 12 October 2026 for icons that are visible and centered): both sizes are even on purpose, because the old 15px icon in a 26px tile left 5.5px on each side and could not sit on the pixel grid. On mount the cards cascade from left to right (`--ordem` comes from `:nth-child`, up to four cards, and `--entrada` is the resulting delay, which `MedidorOcupacao` also reads to grow its bar) and each icon draws its own strokes: a layout effect sets `pathLength="1"` on the children of the lucide SVG so one `stroke-dashoffset` keyframe fits any icon. The animations sit on the label, the value and the context, not on the card itself, because the grid shows `--borda` through its 1px gaps and a fading card would flash that color. The label row reserves two lines (`min-height: 2.25rem`), so a label that wraps does not push its value below the neighbours; between 901px and 1000px of `resumo` width the card tightens its side padding and the label gap, which is what keeps "Tempo médio de execução" on one line at 1280px with the menu open. The hover only lights the card and rings the tile: the cards are not clickable, so do not add a lift or a pointer (the exceptions are "Consultas lentas" and "Conexões ativas" on the dashboard, described with the query list below). Connections use `Plug`, the counterpart of the `Unplug` shown when there is no connection.
* The dashboard blocks enter one after the other (the user asked on 12 October 2026 to animate every card of the screen): `.blocos > *` in `PaginaDashboard.module.css` takes `--bloco` from `:nth-child` and starts one page step after the previous block, on top of the page entrance of `LayoutAplicacao`. The activity chart draws over 900ms with `ease-out` (`--duracao-leitura` in `GraficoAtividade.module.css`), the same time `useContagem` takes, so the line and the numbers settle together; the report charts keep 480ms. In `TabelaConsultas` (shared with Resumo) the rows cascade with `item-em-cascata` and the share bars grow from zero, but only while `entrando` is true: the bar of the last row turns it off on `animationend`. Do not make those classes permanent, because React moves the `tr` nodes when the table is sorted and a moved node restarts its CSS animation, which replayed the entrance on some rows only.
* The value of `IndicadorNumerico` wraps `.` and `,` in a `.separador` span with negative side margins (the user said on 12 October 2026 that the comma of "Tempo médio de execução" sat too far from the digits): in Geist Mono the separator takes a whole digit cell. It is split from the formatted string, so the hidden copy for screen readers stays plain text.
* The dashboard entrance is one sequence (the user asked on 12 October 2026 for an animation when entering the screen): the frames settle first (`assentarBloco` in `PaginaDashboard.module.css`, 16px and `--duracao-expressiva`), then the data plays from top to bottom. `useEntradaCoordenada` holds the three first answers until all of them arrive, or 600ms at most, and then releases the indicators, the chart and the table 160ms apart; `aguardarEtapa` shows a held block as still loading. Each block delays its skeleton by the longest wait of its stage (`--espera-esqueleto`, read by `Esqueleto.module.css`, 300ms everywhere else), so a normal load never flashes a skeleton and a slow one still gets it. With reduced motion nothing is held. A change of period plays the same sequence again, with the skeletons in place of the old data (the user asked on 12 October 2026 for the skeleton on the dashboard; until then the previous period stayed on screen dimmed): `useDadosPorChave` (in `useDadosAssincronos.ts`) tags each answer with the period it was asked for and gives `null` while the tag is not the requested one, so not even the first render after the click shows the old numbers, and `useEntradaCoordenada` takes the period as its `rodada` and starts over when it changes. From the first change on, `trocouDePeriodo` sets `--espera-esqueleto` to `0ms`, so the skeletons come at once; only the first load keeps the wait. The table skeleton is `EsqueletoTabelaConsultas`, in the same file and with the same `MolduraDaTabela` as `TabelaConsultas`, so the header and the sort buttons stay and the five column widths are identical. `DashboardConectado` owns the ordering (`ordenacao` and `aoOrdenar`, optional in both components; Resumo leaves them out and the table keeps its own state), which is what keeps the chosen column through the skeleton. The skeleton rows repeat the loaded ones: two lines of text and one row with a single line as a table (747px against 748px for the ten queries at 1440px), every other row with one more line as cards, and a third line below 420px of `consultas`; `EsqueletoIndicadores` follows the one-column card of `IndicadorNumerico` below 420px of `resumo` (381px against 380px at 390px). Measure both again after changing a column or the card. Do not take screenshots of this screen with `captureBeyondViewport` in a headless Chrome: the capture replayed the chart reading and clipped the menu, none of which was in the DOM. `ResumoIndicadores` passes `revelar={false}` to `ConteudoAssincrono`, because the frame of the grid belongs to the content and fading it made the frame blink when the skeleton gave way.
* The activity chart is revealed by a reading line (`FioDeLeitura`, a hairline with a short trail that sweeps from left to right), not by the Recharts animation, which is off. The line and the area are uncovered by a `clip-path` keyframe on `.serie` and the hairline moves by `translateX`: two CSS animations with the same duration and easing, mounted in the same commit (the hairline goes in through `Customized`, so it sits inside the SVG and gets the plot `offset`), which is what keeps the edge of the cut exactly under the hairline. Do not move the hairline to an HTML overlay or turn the Recharts animation back on: both start a frame or two apart from the cut. `AreaChart` is keyed by the data (`leitura`), so a new period replays the reading instead of morphing between two time bases. To look at a frame of it, pause `document.getAnimations()` from a timer, or slow them with `playbackRate`.
* The SQL cell of `TabelaConsultas` is `ConsultaSql` (`src/componentes/comum/`, asked by the user on 12 October 2026): `TrechoSql` plus a button that shows the whole query and one that copies it. `TrechoSql` alone stays in `DestaquesRelatorio` and in `ConsultasComparadas`, where the 38% column is too narrow to give up the width of two buttons without truncating almost every query. The toggle exists only while the text is cut, which `ConsultaSql` measures on the `code` element (`scrollHeight` against `clientHeight`, again on resize and when the fonts load); the height animation runs on the wrapper (`moldura`), not on the `code`, so that measurement is never taken in the middle of it. Clicking the cut text only opens it: closing is the button's job, so a double click still selects a word. The copied text is the raw SQL, with no demo notice, on purpose (`REQUISITOS.md`). The copy button is hidden until the row is hovered or focused by the keyboard on devices with hover and always visible on touch; the rule is `:has(:focus-visible)`, not `:focus-within`, because a mouse click leaves the focus on the button and it stayed on screen after the confirmation (the user reported it on 12 October 2026); the confirmation is the check icon plus a `role="status"`, as in `DestaquesRelatorio`, not a notification. The copy icon must never come back on screen after the check (the user reported it twice on 12 October 2026): `faseCopia` goes from `CONFIRMANDO` (2.4s, button forced visible) to `LIBERADA`, in which the check stays and the button follows the hover again, and only the `transitionend` of its opacity reaching 0 puts the copy icon back (`PARADA`). Swapping the icon on the timer made it flash during the 120ms fade. Where the button is always visible (no hover) there is no fade to wait for, so the timer swaps it directly. In the card layout the table moves the two buttons to the foot of the card through `classeAcoes` (absolute against the `tr`), because beside the text they took a third of a 320px card. Checking the copy through the Chrome extension must not touch the clipboard of the machine: replace `navigator.clipboard.writeText` in the page first and read the text from there.
* The first theme is dark: `public/tema-inicial.js` applies it before React mounts (no inline script). If `CHAVE_TEMA` changes, update that file too.
* A `Botao` with `carregando` is `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so it keeps focus while the request runs. Do not switch it back.
* The palette is neutral black or white plus blue (decided with the login screen). Three blue roles, do not merge them: `--destaque` is for text, icons, borders and focus on a surface; `--acao`, `--acao-forte` and `--acao-contraste` are for filled buttons (white text needs the deeper blue); `--grafico-1` is for chart marks and was validated against both surfaces with the dataviz palette validator.
* The notification card has its own tokens in `temas.css` (`--notificacao-fundo`, `--notificacao-borda`, `--notificacao-realce` for the hover of the close button). `Notificacao.tsx`, its CSS and `ProvedorNotificacoes` are otherwise the same files as in OrbitWeb (plus one print rule that hides the notification area); the user asked on 11 October 2026 for the card to stand out like there, and on the black palette `--superficie-elevada` with `--borda` gave about half of OrbitWeb's edge contrast. The dark values (`#1b1b1b` on `#272727`) reproduce OrbitWeb's ratios (card against page 1.15, border against card 1.16); the light ones equal `--superficie-elevada`, `--borda` and `--superficie-suave`. Other floating surfaces keep `--superficie-elevada`.
* The brand mark (`MarcaOrynt`) is flat, on purpose (the user asked for no 3D on 11 October 2026): a donut chart that forms the "O" of the name, with one slice pulled out of the ring. The ring takes `--marca-corpo` (the text color) and the slice `--marca-destaque` (the accent blue), both aliases in `temas.css`, so it follows the theme. The slice slides out on the login entrance (`animada`) and snaps back into the ring when a link around the mark is hovered. The two shapes are filled paths on a 32-unit grid, not strokes (redrawn on 11 October 2026, when the user said the mark looked pixelated on a 100% scale monitor): the ring is 270° of a donut centred on (15, 17) with radii 15 and 8, the slice is the missing quarter moved 2 units up and right. Every straight edge and every extreme of the circles falls on a whole unit, so at 32px, the size used in the shell, the login and the 404 page, the cuts are sharp and only the curves are antialiased: keep whole numbers in the paths and render the mark at 32px or a multiple of it wherever it matters (the splash uses 44). Below 32px `MarcaOrynt` switches to a second drawing on a 24-unit grid (`DESENHO_COMPACTO`: centre (11, 13), radii 11 and 6, same 2-unit offset), because the 32-unit paths scaled to 24px put every cut on a fraction of a pixel; the header in drawer mode uses it at 24px (the user said on 12 October 2026 that the 32px mark looked too big beside the header icons on smaller screens), inside a link the size of `--alvo-toque`. `--fatia-encaixada` is that same 2-unit offset: change the two together. `public/icone.svg` is a static copy of the same two paths on the same 32-unit grid, with no tile behind it (the user asked on 11 October 2026 for the tab icon to equal the mark in the shell). It cannot read the theme tokens, so it repeats the values of `--texto` and `--destaque` and picks them with `prefers-color-scheme` (ring `#fafafa` and slice `#5b9cff` on dark browser chrome, `#0f1115` and `#1d5bd8` on light): it follows the browser's scheme, not the app theme, so the ring always contrasts with the tab. Change the paths and those four colors together with the mark and `temas.css`.
* A `Botao` that moves its arrow on hover must select it as a direct child (`.enviar > svg:last-child`, `.entrar > svg:last-child`). The spinner of the loading state is an `svg` inside a `span`, so the descendant selector also matched it, and with the mouse still over the button after the click the spinner stopped turning and slid 3px sideways instead (the user reported it on the recovery buttons on 12 October 2026; the login had it too). `girar` now declares its `from` keyframe as well, so a transform applied to the spinner from outside can no longer replace the rotation.
* A screen that throws or whose lazy chunk fails no longer leaves the page blank (asked on 20 October 2026). Two `LimiteDeErro` catch it: one in `LayoutAplicacao`, inside the `div` keyed by the path, so the menu and the header keep working and the next screen starts clean (`FalhaNaTela`, `EstadoErro` with the new `icone` and `acoes` props), and one around the whole `Aplicacao`, outside the router and the providers, for anything above the shell (`TelaDeFalha`, the old look of the 404 page with a psql `ERRO:` line; it may not use router or provider hooks). The only action is "Recarregar a página": a `lazy()` whose import failed keeps failing until the page reloads, so a "Tentar novamente" would not work. `falhaDeTela.ts` tells a module that did not arrive (the messages of Chrome, Firefox and Safari) from a render error, never shows the error text and gives the tab a title of its own. To look at them, throw from a page or from `RotaProtegida` for a moment; the Vite gotcha below also triggers the first one in development.
* The connection form and the dashboard use container queries (`formulario`, `resumo`, `consultas`) because the modal and the sidebar change the space actually available. The query table turns into cards below 780px of container width.
