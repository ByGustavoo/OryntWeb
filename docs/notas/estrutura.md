# Estrutura detalhada

O que cada pasta de `src/` guarda, arquivo por arquivo. O resumo fica no `CLAUDE.md`.

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `src/api/` | `clienteHttp` (the only place that makes requests), `rotasApi` (every API path), `ErroApi`, `tratamentoErros`, the `fetch` transport |
| `src/dados/simulacao/` | API simulator: `transporteSimulado` (routes, with `:id` segments), `manipuladores/`, `series` (deterministic dashboard data), `relatorios` (deterministic report data, built on `series`), `consultasFicticias` (the ten queries the dashboard lists plus the 32 secondary ones), `consultas` (the complete list of Consultas SQL › Todas, built on `series`, and its search, filters, ordering and paging), `historicoConsulta` (the intervals of one query for Consultas SQL › Histórico, built on `consultas` and on the activity series), `planosExecucao` (the execution plans of Consultas SQL › Planos de execução, their versions and the suggested indexes), `trocasDePlano` (the queries whose plan changed and when, shared by the plans and the history), `visoes` (the nine views of Objetos do banco › Views, their dependencies, the queries that read them and the last refresh, taken from the history of the REFRESH), `indices` (the 40 indexes of Objetos do banco › Índices, their tables and writes, their reads taken from the simulated plans, and the suggestions of the plans), `tabelas` (the 19 tables of Objetos do banco › Tabelas on top of the tables of `indices`, with their maintenance, partitions, autovacuum settings and daily growth, their reads taken from the simulated plans and from `indices`), `funcoes` (the 13 functions of Objetos do banco › Funções and the triggers that call them, with calls taken from the writes of `indices` and from the executions of the queries, so the numbers match Tabelas), `sessoes` (the snapshot of `pg_stat_activity` of Usuários e permissões › Sessões, built on the sessions per role of `papeis` and on the queries of `consultasFicticias`, with the designed lock chain, long query and forgotten transaction repeating in clock windows), `papeis` (the 15 roles of Usuários e permissões › Papéis, plus the role of the connection when it is not one of them, with the owners of Tabelas, Views and Funções and the sessions that add up to the active connections of the dashboard), `permissoes` (the ACL of every object of Permissões: the database, the three schemas, the tables, views and functions of the other screens with the same owners and the sequences of their keys, the grants and the default privileges, on the roles of `papeis`), `alteracoesSimuladas` (the role and permission changes made through the Orynt, per connection, in `localStorage`, replayed over the generated data of Papéis, Permissões, Sessões and the owners of Tabelas, Views and Funções), `credenciaisDemonstracao` (the demo account, the initial administrator), `contasSimuladas` (the Orynt accounts, their sessions, invites and login attempts, in `localStorage`), `auditoriaSimulada` (the audit log, the 5.000 newest events), `autorizacaoSimulada` (the session account and the permission of each route, the `403` and its `ACESSO_NEGADO` event), `convitesSimulados` (the simulated inbox of invites, in memory), `hashSenhaSimulada` (PBKDF2 and token hashes), `acessoSimulado` (the login cookie, only the account and session ids) and `sessaoSimulada` (the connection cookie), `recuperacaoSimulada` (recovery codes, attempts and reset tokens, kept in memory only, plus the simulated inbox), `conexoesSalvasSimuladas` (the saved list, in `localStorage`), `controles` (dev switches) |
| `src/servicos/` | `servicoAdministracao` (accounts and audit), `servicoAutenticacao`, `servicoConexao`, `servicoConsultas`, `servicoDashboard`, `servicoObjetos`, `servicoRelatorios`, `servicoSistema`, `servicoUsuarios`: a thin layer over `clienteHttp` |
| `src/modelos/` | DTO types, enums and Portuguese labels (`rotulos.ts`); `administracao.ts` holds the accounts, the permission codes, the audit events and `PROBLEMAS_ADMINISTRACAO`; `alteracoes.ts` holds `OperacaoDTO`, the structured operations of the role and permission changes, `AlteracaoDTO` and `PROBLEMAS_ALTERACAO` |
| `src/regras/` | Pure rules with tests: `validacaoAcesso`, `falhaAcesso`, `recuperacaoSenha` (the three steps, the code, the new password and the resend wait), `forcaSenha` (the heuristic behind the strength meter), `falhaRecuperacao`, `validacaoConexao`, `urlConexao`, `falhaConexao`, `falhaDeTela` (whether a crash is a module that did not arrive or a render error, and the texts of both error screens), `conexoesSalvas`, `periodoRelatorio` (closed calendar periods, their limits and their Portuguese text), `destaquesRelatorio` (the sentences of the report and the text that is copied), `exportacaoRelatorio` (the tables, the CSV, JSON and Markdown files and their names), `comparacaoRelatorio` (the pair of periods of Comparação, its shortcuts, every difference between two reports and the sentences of "O que mudou"), `buscaConsultas` (the terms of a search, the match and the stretches to mark in the SQL), `filtrosConsultas` (the filters, the ordering and the page of Consultas SQL › Todas, read from the history state, and the texts of the count), `exportacaoConsultas` (the CSV of the filtered list, on the writers of `exportacaoRelatorio`), `historicoConsulta` (the selection of Histórico read from the history state, the texts of each interval per period, the points in evidence, the ordering of the interval table and the sentences of its "Destaques"), `exportacaoHistorico` (the CSV of the intervals of one query), `paginacaoLocal` (the pages of a list that is filtered in the browser, used by Views, Índices and Tabelas), `planoExecucao` (the selection of Planos de execução, the names and measures of each step, the rows of the tree, the sentences of "Diagnóstico" and the text and JSON copies), `comparacaoPlanos` (how each table is read, the steps that changed, the sentences and the measures of the comparison of two plans), `visoes` (the situation of a materialized view, the views without reads, the filters and the ordering of Views read from the history state, the readable definition, the sentences of the detail and the attention points), `indices` (the situation of an index, the duplicates and the redundant ones, the filters and the ordering of Índices read from the history state, the cost of keeping an index, the attention points, the command of the detail and the text of the collection date), `tabelas` (the situations of a table, the filters and the ordering of Tabelas read from the history state, the attention points, the maintenance commands and their batch, the growth summary and the scale of its chart, the autovacuum settings in words and the short column types), `funcoes` (the situations of a function, the measured state, the filters and the ordering of Funções read from the history state, the attention points, the `ALTER … SET search_path` and `DROP` commands and their batch, the trigger firing in words and the sub-millisecond time format), `papeis` (the situations of a role, the password state, the use state, the filters and the ordering of Papéis read from the history state, the attributes reached through SET ROLE and the inherited groups, the attention points, the `ALTER ROLE` and `DROP ROLE` commands and their batch, the quoting of a role name and its initials (`iniciaisDoPapel`)), `permissoes` (the effective access of a role to an object and its origins, the seven situations, the attention points, the rows of the three modes, the comparison and the most similar role, the filters read from the history state, the `GRANT`, `REVOKE` and `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES` commands and their batch, and the ids `banco:` and `esquema:`), `sqlAlteracoes` (the SQL of each `OperacaoDTO`, the same text the review shows and the API runs), `aplicacaoAlteracoes` (applies operations to `ListaPapeisDTO` and `ListaPermissoesDTO`, shared by the review and the simulator), `capacidadeAlteracoes` (what the role of the connection may do, the reason when it may not, and the name to type), `efeitoAlteracoes` (the sentences of "O que muda"), `inversasAlteracoes` (the operations that undo a change), `descricaoAlteracoes` (the sentences of each operation), `formularioPapel` (the role form, its four templates, validation and operations, and the membership form), `formularioConcessao` (the GRANT, REVOKE and default privilege forms), `exclusaoPapel` (what holds a role and its drop), `falhaAlteracao` (the refused change in words), `historicoAlteracoes` (the filters of Alterações), `linhaDoTempo` (the days of a timeline and their names), `permissoesConta` (the catalog of account permissions, the profiles, `telasDoOrynt`, `avaliarMudancaDeConta` and the reasons a permission is blocked), `contas` (the situation of an Orynt account, the filters of Administração › Usuários, the account form and the consequences of a deletion), `auditoria` (the labels of the audit, `filtrarEventos`, the filters and the sentence of each event), `inicialUsuario` (the initial of the account circle), `sessoes` (the situations of a session, the shown state, the blocking chains, the filters and the ordering of Sessões read from the history state, the distribution by application and origin, the attention points, the `pg_cancel_backend` and `pg_terminate_backend` commands and their batch, the timeline and the `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` suggestion shared with Papéis) |
| `src/provedores/` | `ProvedorTema`, `ProvedorNotificacoes`, `ProvedorAutenticacao` (signed-in user with profile and permissions, checked on load, refreshed by `atualizarUsuario` on every screen opened, dropped by `aoPerderOAcesso` of `clienteHttp` on a `401` `/problems/unauthenticated` from any route, and sign-out), `ProvedorConexao` (active connection, saved connections and the reconnection on load), `ProvedorNovaConexao` (opens the connection modal; mounted by `LayoutAplicacao`), `ProvedorAlteracoes` (opens the change modal from any screen with `abrir(pedido)`, exposes `versao` and `ultima` and shows the undo notification; mounted by `LayoutAplicacao`) |
| `src/ganchos/` | `usePermissao` (whether the signed-in account has a permission), `useRegistroDaTela` (posts the screen opened to `POST /auditoria/acessos`, once per path), `useFiltrosGuardados` (generic filters in the history state, used by the two Administração screens), `useEscolhaDePermissoes` (the profile and permissions being edited, with the locks and the profile switch), `useFluxoAcesso` (the login form state machine), `useFluxoRecuperacaoSenha` (the three steps of the password recovery), `useSegundosRestantes` (the resend countdown), `useFluxoConexao` (the connection form state machine), `useCenaMonitoramento` (clock of the login illustration), `usePeriodoRelatorio` (the report period kept in the history state, shared by Resumo and Exportação), `useComparacaoRelatorio` (the two periods of Comparação, kept the same way), `useFiltrosConsultas` (the period, the search, the filters, the ordering and the page of Consultas SQL › Todas, kept the same way), `useSelecaoHistorico` (the query and the period of Consultas SQL › Histórico, kept the same way), `useVerHistorico` (the navigation to Histórico from the other tables), `useSelecaoPlano` (the query and the plan version of Planos de execução, kept the same way), `useVerPlano` (the navigation to Planos de execução from the other screens), `useFiltrosVisoes` (the search, the filters and the ordering of Objetos do banco › Views, kept in the history state), `useFiltrosIndices` (the same for Objetos do banco › Índices), `useFiltrosTabelas` (the same for Tabelas), `useFiltrosFuncoes` (the same for Funções), `useFiltrosPapeis` (the same for Papéis), `useFiltrosSessoes` (the same for Sessões, plus the automatic refresh), `useFiltrosAlteracoes` (the search, the filters and the page of Alterações, kept in the history state), `useFiltrosPermissoes` (the mode, the selection, the filters and the page of Permissões, kept in the history state, and `lerLinhaDaChegada`), `usePermissoesSobDemanda` (asks `GET /usuarios/permissoes` only when the first detail of Tabelas, Views or Funções opens, for "Quem acessa"), `useVerObjeto` (the navigation to the detail of a table, a function, a view, an index or a role on another screen, to Permissões by role or by object, and `useObjetoDaChegada`, which reads the object to open on arrival), `useImpressao` (the print button of the two report screens), `useLeituraDoGrafico` (the point under the mouse in the timeline charts), `useEntradaCoordenada` (holds the first data of the dashboard and releases its blocks in stages), `useChegadaNaAplicacao` (holds the redirect after a login and starts the transition into the shell), `useDadosAssincronos`, `useContagem` (the numbers that count up on the dashboard), `useConsultaMidia` (a media query, and `useMenuEmGaveta`), `useLarguraElemento` and `useTamanhoElemento` (the measured box of an element), `useTravarRolagem` (locks the page under a modal), `useTituloDocumento` (the tab title), `useArmazenamentoLocal` (`orynt:tema` and `orynt:menu-recolhido`) |
| `src/componentes/` | `administracao/` (`TabelaContas`, `BarraFiltrosContas`, `DetalheConta`, `EditorDePermissoes`, `ModalConta` (create and edit), `ModalExclusaoConta`, `SeloSituacaoConta`, `BotaoProtegido` (visible but blocked with the reason), `PerfisDasContas` (the profiles panel beside the list) and the lazy `ConviteNaCaixaSimulada` for Usuários; `LinhaDoTempoAuditoria`, `BarraFiltrosAuditoria`, `DetalheEvento`, `ResumoDaContaAuditoria` and `SeloResultadoAuditoria` for Auditoria; all on the CSS of `objetos/`), `ui/` (design system; `CaixaMarcacao` is the checkbox; `Dica` is the tooltip of every screen), `layout/` (`SemAcessoNaTela` is the full-page 403 that `LayoutAplicacao` shows in place of the shell, on `TelaDeErroHttp`; `MenuConta` is the account menu in the header: connection in use, connection switcher and sign-out; `FalhaNaTela` is the error of one screen inside the shell; `GuardaDaTela` and `SemAcessoNaTela` show "Sem acesso" for a screen the account may not open), `comum/` (brand, `LimiteDeErro` and `TelaDeFalha`, the full-screen error, `TerminalPsql`, the typed psql window of the 404 and the 403, `MolduraDeErro`, the page frame of the 404, the 403 and `TelaDeFalha`, `TelaDeErroHttp`, that frame with the router brand and the shortcuts, and `LinhaDoTempo`, the day-grouped timeline of Auditoria and Alterações), `login/` (form, demo access note and the animated panel; `roteiroCena.ts` is its deterministic script; `CabecalhoAcesso` and `LinkAcesso.module.css` are shared with the recovery screen), `recuperacao/` (`EtapaEmail`, `EtapaCodigo`, `EtapaNovaSenha`, `MedidorForcaSenha` and the lazy `DemonstracaoRecuperacao`), `conexao/` (`ModalConexao`, `FormularioConexao`, `ListaConexoesSalvas`, `PainelSemConexao`), `dashboard/` (also holds what the report reuses: `GradeIndicadores`, `IndicadorNumerico`, `VariacaoIndicador`, `MedidorOcupacao`, `TabelaConsultas`), `relatorios/` (`BarraPeriodo`, shared by the two report screens; `DestaquesRelatorio`, `IndicadoresRelatorio`, `GraficoEvolucao` and `DistribuicaoDesempenho` for Resumo; `OpcoesExportacao` and `PreviaExportacao` for Exportação; `CabecalhoImpressao`, the header of the printed sheet of Resumo and Comparação; `SeletorComparacao`, `IndicadoresComparacao`, `GraficoComparacao`, `DesempenhoComparado` and `ConsultasComparadas` for Comparação, which also reuses `DestaquesRelatorio` through its `textos` prop; `LeituraDoGrafico` is the reading in the title row of the timeline charts, also used by Histórico; `PassosPeriodo` is the pair of arrows shared by `BarraPeriodo` and `SeletorComparacao`; `EsqueletoRelatorio` holds the skeleton pieces of the three report screens), `consultas/` (`BarraFiltrosConsultas`, `MenuExportarCsv`, `TabelaTodasConsultas` and `DetalheConsulta`, the centered detail modal, for Consultas SQL › Todas; `ConsultaEmAnalise`, `SeletorConsulta`, `IndicadoresHistorico`, `GraficoHistorico` and `TabelaIntervalos` for Consultas SQL › Histórico, which also reuses `MenuExportarCsv` and `DestaquesRelatorio`; `ArvorePlano`, `DetalheEtapa`, `DiagnosticoPlano`, `IndicadoresPlano`, `IndicesSugeridos`, `VersoesPlano`, `MenuCopiarPlano` and `ComparacaoPlanos` for Consultas SQL › Planos de execução, which also reuses `ConsultaEmAnalise`), `objetos/` (`BarraFiltrosVisoes`, `TabelaVisoes` with its skeleton, `DetalheVisao` and `SeloTipoVisao` for Objetos do banco › Views; `BarraFiltrosIndices`, `TabelaIndices` with its skeleton, `DetalheIndice`, `SeloSituacaoIndice` and `SugestoesDeIndices` for Índices; `BarraFiltrosTabelas`, `TabelaDeTabelas` with its skeleton, `DetalheTabela`, `SeloSituacaoTabela` and `GraficoCrescimento` for Tabelas; `BarraFiltrosSessoes`, `ResumoSessoes` (connections, states, distribution and the refresh controls) with its skeleton, `CadeiasDeBloqueio`, `TabelaSessoes` with its skeleton, `DetalheSessao` and `SeloSituacaoSessao` (also `SeloEstadoSessao` and `SeloOrynt`) for Sessões; `BarraPermissoes` (the modes and the selection above the filters), `TabelaPermissoes` (also `TabelaComparacao` and `EsqueletoPermissoes`), `DetalheAcesso`, `PrivilegiosPadrao`, `SeloSituacaoPermissao` (also `SeloNivel`) and `QuemAcessa` (the section of the Tabelas, Views and Funções details) for Permissões;
