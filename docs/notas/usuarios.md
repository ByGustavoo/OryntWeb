# Usuários e permissões: Papéis, Sessões, Permissões e Alterações

* Usuários e permissões › Papéis (`PaginaPapeis`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Usuários e permissões: Papéis", built on 19 October 2026) repeats the shape of Funções: one request, `GET /usuarios/papeis`, with the whole list, filtered, ordered (by name, users before groups) and paged in the browser (`filtrarPapeis`, `paginarLista`), the filters in the history state as `{ papeis }` (`useFiltrosPapeis`, read through `lerFiltrosPapeis`), the detail in a centered `Modal` with arrows, and the same attention panel, shortcuts and batch copy, reusing the components and the CSS modules of `objetos/`. A role is identified by its name (`lerNomeDoPapel`, also accepted by `useObjetoDaChegada`); `PontosDeAtencao` and `NomeObjeto` take `esquema: null` for it. The situations (`situacoesDoPapel`, in urgency order: `SUPERUSUARIO`, a login superuser that is not `inicial`; `VALIDADE_DA_SENHA`, expired or within `DIAS_ATE_VENCER` (7), red only when expired, which is why its badge text and tone come from `rotuloDaSituacaoDoPapel` and `tomDaSituacaoPapel` with the role; `SENHA_MD5`; `PERTO_DO_LIMITE`, 80% of `limiteConexoes`; `SEM_USO`, a login role with no session in 30 days and observed for 30 days, otherwise `EM_OBSERVACAO`; `GRUPO_VAZIO`) are decided in the front from the DTO, and "last use" is `vistoPorUltimo` from the samples of `pg_stat_activity`, because PostgreSQL does not record logins. Attributes are not inherited in PostgreSQL: `participacoesComSetRole` follows the memberships with `assume` (the SET option) to list what a role gains with SET ROLE, and `gruposHerdados` follows the ones with `herda` for the privileges; a login role that can reach a superuser that way gets a red note. No command carries a password: the password commands are `VALID UNTIL` and `\password` for psql. The commands quote a role name that is not a plain identifier (`identificadorSql`). The simulated roles (`dados/simulacao/papeis.ts`) take their objects from `gerarTabelas`, `gerarVisoes` and `gerarFuncoes` by `dono`, and their sessions add up to `conexoesNoInstante` (`sessoesPorPapel`: fixed sessions for the small ones, one more for the role of the connection, the rest to `loja_app`); the role the user typed in the connection modal is added when it is not one of the 15. `papeis.test.ts` checks those sums, that every owner exists and has its objects, that memberships are symmetric and the situation of each designed case. `DonoDoObjeto` puts "Dono" with a link to the role in the detail of Tabelas, Views and Funções (S3), between the description and the badges; the TOAST sentence of Tabelas no longer names the owner. The attention skeleton has `[3, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2]` lines, of which the collapsed panel draws the first three, and measured 414.2px like the loaded panel at 1920px of window, and the skeleton rows 64 to 67px against 64 to 67px loaded: measure again after changing a sentence or a column. The password method shows "SCRAM" in the measures of the detail, because "SCRAM-SHA-256" was cut at 320px; the full name is in its title and in the sentence below. Below 560px of `filtros-papeis` the type and the attribute selects share a row and the situation one takes its own; the situation select is 16rem on wide screens, because "Senha vencida ou a vencer" was cut at 13rem.
* Usuários e permissões › Sessões (`PaginaSessoes`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Usuários e permissões: Sessões", built on 19 October 2026) is the snapshot of `pg_stat_activity`: one request, `GET /usuarios/sessoes`, with every process of the server, filtered, ordered and paged in the browser (`filtrarSessoes`, `paginarLista`), the filters in the history state as `{ sessoes }` (`useFiltrosSessoes`, read through `lerFiltrosSessoes`), the detail in a centered `Modal` with arrows, and the attention panel, shortcuts and batch copy of the other lists, on the CSS modules of `objetos/`. Every duration is measured from `geradoEm`, never from the browser clock (`tempoNoEstadoMs`, `tempoDaTransacaoMs`, `tempoDaConsultaMs`, written by `formatarTempoDecorrido`). The database filter starts on the database of the connection: `banco` is `BANCO_DA_CONEXAO` (an empty string) until the user picks one, `bancoEfetivo` resolves it, `null` is "Qualquer banco", and a process without a database (replication, checkpointer) shows under any database. Only `CLIENTE` sessions show by default; `processos` (an `Interruptor`, the switch added to `componentes/ui/` for this screen) shows `REPLICACAO`, `AUTOVACUUM` and `SERVIDOR` too. The connection count is `CLIENTE` plus `REPLICACAO` (`contarConexoes`), the number of the Dashboard card and of the sum of Papéis. The situations (`situacoesDaSessao`, in urgency order: `BLOQUEANDO`, someone waits for it and it does not wait itself, so the middle of a queue is only `ESPERANDO_BLOQUEIO`; `ESPERANDO_BLOQUEIO`, a `Lock` wait of 30 s or more; `OCIOSA_EM_TRANSACAO`, labelled "Transação parada" because the state badge already says "Ociosa em transação"; `CONSULTA_LONGA`, 5 min; `TRANSACAO_LONGA`, 30 min) never apply to the session of Orynt (`doOrynt`) or to a server process (`apontavel`). The attention panel cites each session once, in its first situation, and adds the S4 suggestion at the end. The blocking chains (`cadeiasDeBloqueio`) hang each waiter under the first pid of `bloqueadaPor` and start at the sessions that block without being blocked; `CadeiasDeBloqueio` shows only when there is one. A running query gets `pg_cancel_backend` and `pg_terminate_backend`, a stopped session only the second (`comandosDaSessao`); the batch takes `comandoDaSituacao`. PIDs are written with `String`, never `formatarNumero`, which put a thousands separator in them. The automatic refresh (`automatica`, kept in the history state, `paginaAposMudanca` keeps the page for it) is a timer re-armed after each answer in `ListaConectada`, paused while a detail is open or the tab is hidden (`usePaginaVisivel`); `useDadosAssincronos` keeps the previous answer on screen while the next one loads, so the open row and the focus stay. The S4 suggestion (`sugestaoDeTempoOcioso`: 3 or more `ociosasEmTransacao30Dias`, no `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` on the role or the server) is shared by Sessões and the "Configurações próprias" of the Papéis detail, which also gained "Ver as N sessões em Sessões" (`useVerObjeto().sessoesDoPapel`, with `banco: null` and `processos` on for a replication role). The simulated sessions (`dados/simulacao/sessoes.ts`) are the sessions of `gerarPapeis` per role (called with `comObjetos` false, because the owned objects made the test take 90 s), so their count equals the Dashboard and Papéis at any instant: the designed ones (the idle `loja_app` transaction holding a row of `pedidos`, the `fila_pedidos` waiter behind it and the two tuple waiters behind that one, the long `loja_analise` report and its forgotten transaction, `suporte_dba` on psql in the `postgres` database, the `replicador` walsender) go first, the Orynt session belongs to the role of the connection, and `loja_app` takes the remainder as pool sessions whose state changes every minute. The episodes repeat in clock windows (`inicioDaJanela`: 20 min for the block, 10 for the long query, 60 for the forgotten transaction), so the times grow between two refreshes, and the lock waits cycle under the `statement_timeout` of each role. `sessoes.test.ts` checks the sums for every 3 hours of a week and four connection roles, unique pids, that every `consultaId` carries the SQL of the list and that the designed cases hold in every one of those instants. The table puts the free width in the query column (`td.celulaConsulta` with `width: 100%`, `td.celulaSessao` at 16rem): with the shared `celulaVisao` taking the rest, the SQL got 60px at 1920px (seen on 19 October 2026). Below 1320px of `filtros-sessoes` the search takes its own row. In the card layout the secondary line of a numeric cell wraps (`.celulaNumerica .textoSecundario`), because "desde 19 de out, 23:28", the start of a session from the day before, ran 4px past 320px (seen on 20 October 2026); the number above it stays on one line.
* Usuários e permissões › Permissões (`PaginaPermissoes`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Usuários e permissões: Permissões", built on 19 October 2026) is one request, `GET /usuarios/permissoes`, with the raw ACL of every object (`aclexplode`, without the owner's own entries), the roles with their memberships and `pg_default_acl`; the front computes everything. `indexarPermissoes` (`regras/permissoes.ts`) builds the index once per answer (the inherited groups of each role with their path, the read groups, the missing default privileges and a cache of `acessoDe`), so keep the index in a `useMemo` on the DTO. The effective access follows PostgreSQL: a superuser gets everything, the owner and whoever inherits the owner role get everything, and the rest is the grants to the role, to the groups it inherits (`herda` only; a membership without it shows as a note with "Ver …" above the list) and to `PUBLIC`. The origins of each privilege are sorted direct, then by group depth, then `PUBLIC`. Three modes share the state `{ permissoes: { modo, papel, objeto, comparado, … } }` (`useFiltrosPermissoes`); a change of mode resets the list filters (`aplicarMudancaPermissoes`), a change of selection keeps them. `resolverSelecao` in the page falls back to the connection role, to the first table (`objetoInicial`) and to the most similar role (`papelMaisParecido`, Jaccard over the own privileges, no superusers). In "Por objeto" a role that only gets `PUBLIC` is not a row of its own: it is in the `PUBLIC` row, or every function would list all the roles. The open detail is a row key (`aberta`), looked up in the unfiltered rows, so a point of attention can open a row while the selection changes in the same click (`levarAPagina` moves to its page once the row exists); do not close the detail from an effect when the row is missing, because the router applies the new selection in a transition and the first render still has the old rows. Object ids are `banco:<nome>`, `esquema:<nome>` and the ids of Tabelas, Views and Funções (`lerIdDoObjetoPermissao`). The Acesso and Origem cells take their width from a `min-width` on the content (`larguraDoAcesso`, `larguraDaOrigem`, `larguraDoLado`), because the 100% name column squeezed them to the min-content of the text ("CONNECT, TEMPORARY" wrapped at 130px), and the min-widths drop to 0 in the card layout. The mode control is `GrupoOpcoes` above 280px of `filtros-permissoes` and a select below it (both in the DOM), because the three options needed 252px and had 246 at 320px; below 420px the two compare selects stack beside the swap button, whose icon turns 90°. The sentences of `PontosDeAtencao` got `overflow-wrap: anywhere` with this screen, because `public.arquivar_pedidos_antigos(integer)` pushed the page 13px sideways at 320px. The attention skeleton has `[2, 3, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2]` lines, of which the collapsed panel draws the first three, and measured 414.2px like the loaded panel at 1920px of window, and the skeleton rows 66.5 and 67px like the loaded ones: measure again after changing a sentence or a column. "Quem acessa" (S2) is a render prop (`quemAcessa`) of `DetalheTabela`, `DetalheVisao` and `DetalheFuncao`, filled by each page with `QuemAcessa` and `usePermissoesSobDemanda(aberta !== null)`, which asks the permissions once, when the first detail opens. The simulated grants (`dados/simulacao/permissoes.ts`) are declared as rules over the objects (`concessoesFicticias`); `permissoes.test.ts` checks that the owners equal the other screens, that every grantee exists and every grantor can grant, and that the attention points are exactly the seven designed cases.
* Papéis and Permissões change the database since 20 October 2026 (`REQUISITOS.md`, "Usuários e permissões: administração e Alterações"); every other screen still only reads, and its commands still say so. One route, `POST /usuarios/alteracoes`, takes a list of `OperacaoDTO` and runs it in one transaction; the front never sends SQL. The text the review shows, the text copied and the text the API returns are the same `comandosDasOperacoes`: the commands that Papéis and Permissões already offered (`comandosDoPapel`, `comandoDaSituacao`, `formasDeTirar`, `operacoesParaConceder`, `sugestaoDeTempoOcioso`) now carry their `operacoes` and derive the text from them, so a new suggested command needs its operation, or `operacoes: null` when it cannot run (the `\password` of the MD5 case, which becomes "Trocar a senha"). A REVOKE of a grant made by someone other than the owner carries `GRANTED BY` (`concedentesAlheios`), because PostgreSQL only revokes in the name of the grantor: before this, the copied `REVOKE UPDATE ON TABLE public.produtos FROM leitura` would not have removed the grant of `migracao`. Every change opens `ModalAlteracao` through `useAlteracoes().abrir`: the provider lives in `LayoutAplicacao`, keys the modal by opening (so a form never keeps the previous one, and the password field dies with it), reloads `GET /usuarios/papeis` and `GET /usuarios/permissoes` on open for the review, and after a success bumps `versao`, which Papéis, Permissões, Sessões and Alterações have in the dependencies of their fetch. Papéis keeps the open role by name (`nomeAberto`) and follows a rename or a drop through `ultima`. The password never leaves the form state: `verificadorScram` (`utilitarios/scram.ts`, WebCrypto, tested against `node:crypto`) runs on "Revisar", and `ocultarSenhas` swaps the verifier for `OCULTA` before anything is stored. The inverse operations are computed in the front on execute (`inversasDe`, with the state loaded for the review) and sent with the change; a change that creates a role is undone by one `EXCLUIR_PAPEL` with `DROP OWNED`. The simulator keeps the changes per connection id and replays them on every read (`papeisComAlteracoes`, `permissoesComAlteracoes`, `donoComAlteracoes`, `renomeacoesAplicadas`), so the generated data never changes: a dropped role's sessions go to `loja_app` and a renamed role keeps its designed sessions under the new name, and `alteracoes.test.ts` checks the sums, the replay, the refusals and that applying a change and its inverses gives back the same Papéis and Permissões. The saved demo connection may use a plain role (`app`), which cannot change anything: every button is then blocked with its reason and Papéis shows a note; connect as `postgres` to try the flows. The review of a change that reaches PUBLIC takes PUBLIC as the starting point of a new role, or every new role would "gain" CONNECT and EXECUTE. In the Group select of the membership form the groups come before the users.
* `CadeiasDeBloqueio` (asked on 20 October 2026, "hoje tá só uma linha") opens each chain with a sentence (`resumirCadeia`: levels and the longest wait) and draws each session as a card of three rows: PID, role, application, state and how many sessions it holds; the running SQL; and what it waits for (`bloqueioEsperado`, the lock not granted, "que a 17264 segura") and what it holds (`bloqueiosSegurados`, two at most). The time sits in a right column with its word ("parada", "rodando", "na espera"), aligned across the levels because every card ends at the same edge. The connectors are 1px fills in `--guia-arvore` with a dot at each card, measured from `--meio-da-cabeca`; below 520px of `cadeias-bloqueio` the time goes under the first row.
* Papéis has no panorama since 22 October 2026: the attention points are the collapsed strip (`variante="recolhida"`) and `MapaDosPapeis` takes the full width under it. Redone the same day (second review): one bordered list (`.mapa`) with a row per group, sorted by member count, the group button on the left (15rem column) and the members as chips (`FichaDoMembro`) that wrap on the right; the last row, "Sem grupo", has its own background and a dashed top border and holds the login roles whose `membroDe` names no listed role. A chip shows the initials from `iniciaisDoPapel`, the name (which keeps its width; the marks shrink first), the crown of a superuser, "sem herança" (and a dashed border) when the membership has no `herda`, "esta conexão" and, only with `sessoes > 0`, a green dot plus "N sessões"; the other situations are not in the map (they are in the attention strip and the list), only in the chip's `aria-label`. Members with a session come first, then superusers, then by name; a row keeps its chips when they fit in two lines and otherwise shows one line plus "Mais N": `Fichas` renders every chip once (`.medindo`, clipped), counts the lines by `offsetTop` in a layout effect, keeps what fits beside the toggle (`ESPACO_DO_ALTERNADOR_PX`) and measures again when its width changes (ResizeObserver); the collapsed row is `nowrap`, so a long first chip shrinks with an ellipsis instead of pushing the toggle down. Above seven groups the list shows the six largest (four below 640px of the bar, `useLarguraElemento`) and "Mostrar os outros N grupos"; the empty groups share one row ("Sem membros") of group chips. A search field beside the summary filters groups by name (the row then shows every member) and members by name, opens every row and every chip while it has text, and "Com sessão aberta" hides the rows with nobody connected. Checked on 22 October 2026 with 14 groups and 82 users created through `POST /usuarios/alteracoes` in the simulator (`restaurarPapeis()` drops them): 828px against 1316px before, no sideways scroll at 390px. A name that ends in a number takes that number in its initials (`usuario_07` gives `U7`), because "U0" repeated on every chip. The chips have no tooltip on purpose: it covered the summary and the row above. A legend under the list explains the dashed border when a membership without inheritance exists, and below 420px of `mapa-papeis` the "sem herança" text hides and the border plus the legend carry it. Below 640px the group sits above its chips. "Com sessão aberta" filters every row by `sessoes > 0`; the row counts stay full.
