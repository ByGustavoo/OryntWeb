# Objetos do banco: Views, Índices, Tabelas e Funções

* Objetos do banco › Views (`PaginaVisoes`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Objetos do banco: Views") is one request, `GET /objetos/visoes`, with the whole list: unlike Consultas SQL › Todas, the search, the filters and the ordering run in the browser (`filtrarVisoes`), and the filters live in the history state as `{ visoes }` (`useFiltrosVisoes`, read through `lerFiltrosVisoes`; a schema that is not in the loaded list is dropped by an effect in the page). Staleness is measured from `geradoEm` of the DTO, never from the browser clock, against `HORAS_ATE_DESATUALIZAR` (24). "Sem leitura" (`visoesSemLeitura`) is transitive: a view read by a read view, or by a materialized view whose REFRESH ran in the period, counts as read; the REFRESH of a materialized view does not count as a read of itself. The attention shortcuts beside the count ("1 sem dados", "1 desatualizada", "4 sem leitura em 30 dias") toggle the `situacao` filter. Above the list, `PontosDeAtencao` (asked on 18 October 2026) shows the sentences of `pontosDeAtencao`, from the most urgent case to the least (`ordemDosPontos`: a stale materialized view that is still read, one without data, one refreshed that nobody reads, a view without reads); "Abrir" opens the detail and gives the focus back to that button on closing (`abertaPeloPainel` makes `focoAoFechar` return null, so `Modal` falls back to the element that opened it), and the table does not scroll the row of a view opened that way into view (`rolarAteAberta`; it pulled the page down to the list behind the modal, seen on 18 October 2026, and Índices does the same with `rolarAteAberto`), and a case with more views than `maximoDePontosSeparados` becomes one sentence whose "Ver na lista" applies the filter and scrolls to the list. The panel is absent when there is nothing to say, and its skeleton (four items, `LINHAS_POR_FRASE_EM_ESPERA`) measured the same 404px as the loaded panel at 1568px. The readers are the queries of the 30-day list whose text cites the view; the simulator lists them by id (`leitoras` in `visoesFicticias`), and `visoes.test.ts` checks that each one's SQL contains the view name, that the dependencies are symmetric and that the last refresh of `vendas_diarias` comes from the history of its REFRESH. Five secondary queries were rewritten to cite views on 18 October 2026, with the same numbers: the plans of `q-produtos-id` (filter `ativo`), `q-entregas-pendentes` (filter `entregue_em IS NULL`) and `q-avaliacoes-media` (index scan on the materialized view) changed with them, and the other two kept theirs because a plain view is inlined into its table. The definition is shown and copied through `definicaoLegivel`, which drops the leading space `pg_get_viewdef` puts on every line. The detail is `DetalheVisao` (centered `Modal`, `lg`); a view cited in its dependencies opens its own detail (`aoAbrirObjeto`, with a chevron on the items that open), the body scrolls back to the top on each new view (`CorpoDetalhe` is keyed by the id and scrolls its parent, the `Modal` content, in a layout effect), and the footer reads "Fora dos filtros da lista" when the view opened that way is not in the filtered list. The skeleton rows (`EsqueletoVisoes`, same `MolduraDaTabela`) measured 64 and 64.5px, like the loaded ones; measure again after changing a column. The table turns into cards below 860px of `lista-objetos` (four columns, two below 520px), where names break after the dot and each underscore (`NomeObjeto`), not in the middle of a word. The list is paged like Índices (asked on 18 October 2026): `pagina` in `FiltrosVisoes`, `aplicarMudancaVisoes` sends any change but the page back to page 1, `paginarLista` and `paginaDaPosicao` of `paginacaoLocal`, the arrows of the detail change the page, and `Paginacao` only shows with more than one page, so the nine simulated views never show it: to look at it, set `ITENS_POR_PAGINA` to 4 for a moment (checked that way on 18 October 2026).
* Objetos do banco › Índices (`PaginaIndices`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Objetos do banco: Índices") repeats the shape of Views: one request, `GET /objetos/indices`, with the whole list, filtered and ordered in the browser (`filtrarIndices`), the filters in the history state as `{ indices }` (`useFiltrosIndices`, read through `lerFiltrosIndices`; a schema or a table that is not in the loaded list is dropped by an effect in the page, and choosing a schema drops a table of another schema), the detail in a centered `Modal` with arrows and "Fora dos filtros da lista", and the same attention panel and shortcuts. The two pages share `PaginaObjetos.module.css` and the components listed under `objetos/`; `PontosDeAtencao` takes generic items (`ItemDeAtencao`, with an `info` tone for the suggestions line) and `linhasPorFrase` for its skeleton. The front decides the situation of each index (`situacaoDoIndice`, in that order: `INVALIDO`, `DUPLICADO`, `REDUNDANTE`, `EM_USO`, `GARANTE_UNICIDADE`, `EM_OBSERVACAO`, `SEM_USO`) from the structured fields of the DTO (`sobreposicoesDosIndices` compares table, method, columns, included columns and predicate): an index that guarantees uniqueness (`restricao` or `unico`) is never flagged as unused, duplicated or redundant, and an index without reads stays `EM_OBSERVACAO` until 30 days have passed since the latest of `coletaDesde` and `observadoDesde`, measured from `geradoEm`. The cost of keeping an index (S1) is `gravacoesNoIndice`: the inserts plus the updates that are not HOT of its table. The simulated reads come from the simulated plans (`leiturasDosPlanos` in `dados/simulacao/indices.ts`: executions of the current version of each plan times the loops of the step that reads the index) and the writes from the `INSERT` and `UPDATE` queries of each table, so Índices, Planos de execução and Histórico tell one story: `pedidos_cliente_id_idx` is invalid since `marcoDoDegrau`, the instant the plan of `q-pedidos-cliente` changed, and its detail offers "Comparar planos" on that query. `indices.test.ts` checks that every index a plan reads exists on the table the plan names, that the reads match the plans, that no suggested index already exists and the situation of each designed case; a new plan step on an index needs that index in `indicesFicticios`. The list is paged and split in two tabs (asked on 18 October 2026, because the 40 rows plus the suggestions below them took a long scroll): `pagina` and `aba` live in `FiltrosIndices`, in the same history state, and `aplicarMudancaIndices` (called by `useFiltrosIndices`, through `paginaAposMudanca`) sends any change but the page or the tab back to page 1. `paginarLista` (`regras/paginacaoLocal.ts`, shared with Views) cuts the filtered list into pages of `ITENS_POR_PAGINA` (15; it was 25 first and the user found it too long) in the browser, and a page past the end is written back as the last one by an effect in the page; the `Paginacao` of Consultas SQL › Todas sits under the table only when there is more than one page, and a page change scrolls back to the top of the panel. The detail arrows still step through the whole filtered list (`filtrados`), and `percorrer` changes the page when the neighbour is on another one (`paginaDaPosicao`); `TabelaIndices` scrolls the open row into view again when its `indices` change, because the page and the open index may commit in two renders. The tabs are `Abas` and `PainelDaAba` (`componentes/ui/`, the first tabs of the project: `role="tablist"`, roving `tabIndex`, left and right arrows, Home and End, selection follows focus, and the inactive panel is `hidden` and unmounted). An `Aba` takes `quantidade` (a chip, blue on the active tab) and `rotuloCurto`, shown below 420px of the `abas` container ("Existentes", "Sugeridos"; the long labels need about 376px). The tab bar is the first row of the list panel, and its side inset follows the `.topo` padding (`--abas-recuo`). The suggestions tab holds `SugestoesDeIndices`, which no longer has a `Painel` of its own (a visually hidden `h2` and an intro sentence) and reuses the CSS of `IndicesSugeridos`, with an `EstadoVazio` when there is no suggestion. "Ver sugestões" of the last attention line opens that tab, focuses it and scrolls to the panel; "Ver na lista" goes back to the first tab. The attention skeleton has `[3, 2, 2, 2, 2]` lines, of which the collapsed panel draws the first three, and measured 414.2px like the loaded panel at 1920px of window, and the skeleton rows 64 and 64.5px like the loaded ones: measure again after changing a sentence or a column. With a situation filter on, `CopiarComandos` (beside the attention shortcuts, through the `acao` slot of `ResumoDaLista`) copies `loteDeComandos` of the filtered list, one command per line (asked on 18 October 2026). Below 560px of `filtros-indices` the schema and the table share a row (`.emPar`), and `CampoSelecao` takes `textoVazioCurto` ("Esquema", "Tabela"): the trigger is then a container (`campo-selecao`) and swaps to the short text below 149px of content box, which is what "Qualquer esquema" needs with the arrow; the open list keeps the long text, because it is outside that container. A container query measures the content box, not the border box: the first threshold, 175px, swapped the 200px selects of the desktop too. `formatarTamanho` goes up to GB since this screen (the `auditoria` table has 2,1 GB).
* Objetos do banco › Tabelas (`PaginaTabelas`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Objetos do banco: Tabelas", built on 19 October 2026) repeats the shape of Views: one request, `GET /objetos/tabelas`, with the whole list, filtered, ordered (by size, largest first) and paged in the browser (`filtrarTabelas`, `paginarLista`), the filters in the history state as `{ tabelas }` (`useFiltrosTabelas`, read through `lerFiltrosTabelas`), the detail in a centered `Modal` with arrows, and the same attention panel, shortcuts and batch copy (`loteDeComandosDasTabelas` returns the `LoteDeComandos` of Índices, so `CopiarComandos` is shared). A table can have several situations at once (`situacoesDaTabela`, in urgency order: `IDADE_ALTA`, `PRECISA_VACUUM`, `LIDA_INTEIRA`, `ESTATISTICAS_DESATUALIZADAS`, `SEM_CHAVE_PRIMARIA`); the list shows the first and "e mais N pontos", the filter and the shortcuts match any of them. "Lida inteira" compares the rows read (`linhasLidasSequencialmente` against `linhasLidasPorIndice`), not the number of reads: counted by reads no simulated table was flagged, not even `pedidos`, read whole 125 thousand times (seen on 19 October 2026). In the attention panel a table that needs VACUUM and also has stale statistics shows once, in the VACUUM sentence (`comEstatisticasJuntas`), because `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` solves both; the detail still shows both notes. The simulated tables (`dados/simulacao/tabelas.ts`) sit on `tabelasFicticias` of `indices.ts`, which gained `EXCLUSAO` writes (`linhasPorAtualizacao` also scales the deletes: 15 per run of `q-sessoes-limpeza`, whose 1.864 rows per run would have deleted the whole table ten times) and `importacoes_legado`, the table without a primary key; `tabelas.test.ts` checks that the index sizes and reads equal Índices, that the writes come from the queries, that the whole reads come from the `Seq Scan` of the current plan versions, that the growth ends on today's numbers and the situation of each designed case. `auditoria` is partitioned by year, so its primary key became `(id, criado_em)` in Índices too. The growth chart (`GraficoCrescimento`) is a line, not an area, because its y axis does not start at zero (a 1% growth was a flat line from zero): `escalaDoCrescimento` picks round ticks in the unit of the largest value and the unit goes in the title; the x axis uses the `dd/mm` of Histórico with `preserveEnd`, because "21 de set" collided at 390px. Column types are shown short (`tipoCurto`: `timestamptz`), because `timestamp with time zone not null` cut the column name at 760px. The attention skeleton has `[3, 3, 2, 2, 2]` lines, of which the collapsed panel draws the first three, and measured 434.3px like the loaded panel (1920px window); the skeleton rows 64 and 64.5px against 65.1px loaded: measure again after changing a sentence or a column.
* Objetos do banco › Funções (`PaginaFuncoes`, `REQUISITOS.md`, "Objetos do banco: Funções", built on 19 October 2026) repeats the shape of Tabelas: one request, `GET /objetos/funcoes`, with the whole list, filtered, ordered (by schema and name) and paged in the browser (`filtrarFuncoes`, `paginarLista`), the filters in the history state as `{ funcoes }` (`useFiltrosFuncoes`, read through `lerFiltrosFuncoes`; a schema or a language that is not in the loaded list is dropped by an effect in the page), the detail in a centered `Modal` with arrows, and the same attention panel, shortcuts and batch copy. The id of a function is `esquema.nome(argumentos)` (`idDaFuncao`), which is what separates overloads and what `ALTER FUNCTION` and `DROP FUNCTION` take; `lerIdDaFuncao` validates it when it arrives through `abrir`. The usage depends on the `track_functions` of the server (`rastreamento` of the DTO): `uso` is `null` when the server does not measure the function (`linguagemMedida`: none with `NENHUM`, the `sql`, `c` and `internal` ones with `PL`), the list says "Não medida" and such a function is never flagged without calls; with `NENHUM` a note above the list offers the two commands that turn it on (`loteQueMedeAsFuncoes`). A function can have several situations (`situacoesDaFuncao`, in urgency order: `RISCO_DE_SEGURANCA`, `SECURITY DEFINER` without a `search_path=` in `configuracoes`; `PESADA`, 100 ms or more per call; `SEM_CHAMADAS`, measured, zero calls, observed for 30 days like the indexes and with no active trigger, index or view depending on it). `CopiarComandos` takes `seFalhar`, because its default error text talks about indexes; Funções and Tabelas pass their own. `IndiceDTO.funcoes` lists the functions an index depends on (asked on 19 October 2026), and the Índices detail shows them in "Funções que ele usa", only when there is one, with a link to Funções (`aoAbrirFuncao`); in the simulator the link lives in `funcoes` of `indicesFicticios`, which `indicesDaFuncao` of `funcoes.ts` also reads, and `funcoes.test.ts` checks both directions. Times under 1 ms per call go through `formatarTempoPorChamada` (`0,012 ms`; `formatarDuracaoMs` wrote `0,0 ms`). The simulated functions (`dados/simulacao/funcoes.ts`) take their calls from the other screens: the triggers from the writes of `indices.ts` (`definir_atualizado_em` per update of `pedidos` and `estoque`, `validar_estoque` per update of `estoque`, `avisar_sessoes_expiradas` once per run of `q-sessoes-limpeza`, a statement trigger), `calcular_total_pedido` from the executions of `q-pagamento-novo`, whose SQL was rewritten on 19 October 2026 to call it (same numbers, its plan is the generic insert), and `calcular_frete(text, numeric)` from inside it, so its total is the part of `calcular_total_pedido` that is not its own time. `somente_digitos` sustains `clientes_documento_key`, the 41st index of Índices, added for this screen (unique, so it is never flagged). `funcoes.test.ts` checks the trigger calls against Tabelas, that each citing query has the name in its SQL, that no function takes more time per call than the command that calls it and the situation of each designed case. The triggers live in `gatilhosFicticios` and feed both `FuncaoDTO.gatilhos` and the new `TabelaDTO.gatilhos` (`gatilhosDaTabela`), which the Tabelas detail shows in a "Gatilhos" section whose items open the function in Funções (S4). In both details the "Desativado" badge sits under the trigger text (`seloNaLista`), because beside it the name was cut to "clientes_co…" at 320px. In the card layout of the list Situação comes before Tipo, so the type and the three numbers share a row; the card labels of `TabelaObjetos.module.css` wrap now (`white-space: normal` on `td[data-rotulo]::before`), because "Chamadas em 30 dias" ran into the next column at 320px. Below 280px of `filtros-funcoes` the four selects take a row each ("Linguagem" needed 76px of the 66px it had); the language select is 13rem on wide screens, because "Qualquer linguagem" was cut at 184px. The attention skeleton has `[3, 3, 3]` lines and measured 397.5px like the loaded panel at 1920px of window, and the skeleton rows 66.5 and 67px like the loaded ones (the second line of the type cell takes the small font): measure again after changing a sentence or a column.
* The detail of one object screen opens on another through `useVerObjeto`, which navigates with `state: { abrir: id }` (plus `{ indices: { tabela } }` for an index, so Índices opens filtered by its table). Tabelas, Índices, Views and Funções read it once on mount (`useObjetoDaChegada`, validated by `lerTabela` or, for a function id with its arguments, `lerIdDaFuncao`), open the object when the list arrives, move to the page that holds it and call `mudarFiltros`, which replaces the history state and drops `abrir`, so Back does not reopen it. The object opened that way behaves like one opened from the attention panel: the table does not scroll its row into view. The links are the indexes, the views and "Ver em Índices" of the Tabelas detail, the "Tabela" section of the Índices detail (a materialized view goes to Views; its size is the data size, so it says "de dados") and the table items of "Depende de" in Views, the triggers of the Tabelas detail (to Funções) and the triggers, indexes and views of the Funções detail. `papel` opens Papéis the same way, from the owner of a table, a view or a function, and Papéis opens the objects of a role on their screens. `sessoesDoPapel` opens Sessões filtered by a role, and the detail of a session opens its role through `papel`.
* Objetos do banco › Views is the screen once called Visões (22 October 2026): every visible "visão"/"visões" about the database object reads "view"/"views", in the interface, in the simulated object descriptions and in the audit sentences. Code identifiers (`Visao`, `visoes`), the route `/objetos/visoes` and the API keep the old names. Old audit events in `localStorage` keep the screen name they were recorded with.
